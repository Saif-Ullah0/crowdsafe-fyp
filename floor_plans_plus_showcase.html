1. Reduced Power / Range

In real CCTV, ranges are capped to 18.5m - 21.5m. Shooting 36m+ across the building severely degrades pixel density (DORI standard), causing blurry/poor images.

2. Perpendicular Line Boundary

Cameras do not cross the perpendicular line of the public walkway. Each exit dome monitors its own wing up to the central junction, where it hands off tracking seamlessly with a 3m handover zone.

3. Nadir Blind Spot

Because of 32° - 36° downward tilt and 4.2m - 4.8m ceiling mount, there is an authentic 1.8m - 2.1m dead zone directly beneath each camera header before the optical frustum hits the floor.

4. Strict Wall Occlusion

Every optical ray undergoes ray-casting against solid walls. Rays terminate immediately upon wall contact with zero penetration into corner rooms.

5. Fountain Roundabout

A central architectural fountain functions as a pedestrian rotary roundabout. Crowds circulate smoothly around the perimeter lane without 4-way head-on collisions.
