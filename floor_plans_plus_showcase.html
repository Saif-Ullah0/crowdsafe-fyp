1. Real 3D Frustum vs. 2D Triangle

Cameras are mounted at 4.2m - 4.8m ceiling elevation angled downward at 28° - 32° tilt. The 3D rectangular pyramid projects a perspective trapezoid onto the floor with horizontal (HFOV) and vertical (VFOV) boundaries.

2. Nadir Blind Spot Under Mount

Because of downward tilt limits, there is a physical dead zone of 2.8m - 3.2m directly beneath each camera where heads cannot be captured. This is clearly drawn and accounted for in our coverage integrals.

3. Strict Wall Occlusion (Zero Leakage)

Unlike naive 2D wedges, every optical ray undergoes ray-casting against all wall segments. Rays terminate on wall surfaces with zero penetration into corner rooms, casting natural architectural corner shadows.
